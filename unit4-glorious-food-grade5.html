🎯 هدف الدرس

أن يعرّف الطالب أنواع الطعام في المجموعات الخمس، ويتحدث عن طعامه المفضل بجمل بسيطة.

Identify food items in the five food groups and talk about my favourite food using simple sentences.

1

نبدأ الوحدة

Let's start the unit

In this unit we talk about food: what we eat, how we cook it, what is healthy, and how to give advice about food. Food is glorious — it keeps our bodies strong and our minds happy!

3

وصفة: كيف نُحضّر البان كيك

Recipe: How to Make Pancakes

Ingredients: flour, two eggs, milk, a little sugar, butter.

  1. 1First, fetch the ingredients.أولًا، احضر المكوّنات.
  2. 2Then, mix the flour and the eggs.ثم، اخلط الطحين والبيض.
  3. 3Next, add some milk and a little sugar.بعد ذلك، أضف بعض الحليب وقليلًا من السكر.
  4. 4After that, stir the batter.وبعد ذلك، حرّك العجين.
  5. 5Then, fry the pancake in a hot pan.ثم، اقلِ الفطيرة في مقلاة ساخنة.
  6. 6Finally, put some honey on top and serve it hot.أخيرًا، ضع بعض العسل فوقها وقدّمها ساخنة.

القواعد: should و had better والشرط الصفرية

Grammar: Should, Had Better & Zero Conditional

🎯 هدف الدرس

أن يعطي الطالب النصائح باستخدام should و shouldn't و had better، ويستخدم الشرط الصفرية للتحدث عن الحقائق التي تكون صحيحة دائمًا.

Give advice with should / shouldn't / had better, and use the zero conditional to talk about facts that are always true.

1

القاعدة الأولى — should / shouldn't (النصيحة)

Rule 1 — should / shouldn't (advice)

We use should to give good advice, and shouldn't to warn someone. After should / shouldn't we use the base form of the verb (no -s, no -ing).

You should eat more vegetables.

ينبغي أن تأكل خضروات أكثر.

You shouldn't eat too much sugar.

لا ينبغي أن تأكل سكرًا كثيرًا.

She should drink water, not soda.

ينبغي أن تشرب ماءً وليس مشروب غازي.

📌 القاعدة

should + الفعل المجرد (base verb): You should EAT. وليس should eating.

2

القاعدة الثانية — had better

Rule 2 — had better

had better is a little stronger than should. We use it when something is important or urgent. After had better we also use the base verb.

shouldhad better
advice (نصيحة عامة)important advice (نصيحة مهمة)
You should eat breakfast.You had better eat breakfast now.
less strongstronger / more urgent

You had better get your ingredients ready first.

من الأفضل أن تحضّر مكوّناتك أولًا.

You had better wash your hands before you eat.

من الأفضل أن تغسل يديك قبل أن تأكل.

We had better not eat in front of the TV.

من الأفضل ألا نأكل أمام التلفاز.

3

القاعدة الثالثة — الشرط الصفرية

Rule 3 — The zero conditional

We use the zero conditional to talk about facts and results that are ALWAYS true. The rule is very simple: If + present simple, present simple.

If you heat water, it boils.

إذا سخّنت الماء، فإنه يغلي.

If you fry the pancake, it becomes golden.

إذا قليت الفطيرة، تصبح ذهبية اللون.

If you don't eat breakfast, you feel tired.

إذا لم تأكل الإفطار، تشعر بالتعب.

If you mix the eggs and the flour, you make a batter.

إذا خلطت البيض والطحين، تصنع عجينًا.

🚫 خطأ شائع

لا نستخدم will في الشرط الصفرية: ❌ If you heat water, it will boil. ✅ If you heat water, it boils.

4

تدريب على الشرط الصفرية

Zero conditional — practice

  1. 11. Make true sentences: If you eat junk food, you ... (feel tired / get strong)كوّن جملًا صحيحة: إذا أكلت الوجبات السريعة، أنت ... (تشعر بالتعب / تصبح قويًا)
  2. 22. Write two facts about your kitchen at home using If + present simple.اكتب حقيقتين عن مطبخك في البيت باستخدام If + present simple.
  3. 33. Give your partner three pieces of advice with should / shouldn't.أعطِ زميلك ثلاث نصائح باستخدام should / shouldn't.
5

خريطة القواعد السريعة

Quick grammar map

UseFormExample
Adviceshould + base verbYou should drink water.
Warningshouldn't + base verbYou shouldn't skip breakfast.
Important advicehad better + base verbYou had better try it.
Always trueIf + present, presentIf you heat ice, it melts.

  10. 10Omar: Thank you, doctor!عمر: شكرًا لك يا doctor!
  11. 11Doctor: If you eat well, you feel great. Remember: if you heat your milk, it becomes hot!الطبيب: إذا أكلت جيدًا، ستشعر بتحسّن كبير. تذكّر: إذا سخّنت حليبك، يصبح ساخنًا!

الكتابة والمشروع: طعامي المفضل

Writing & Project: My Favourite Food

🎯 هدف الدرس

أن يكتب الطالب مقالًا عن طعامه المفضل، ويقدّم عرضًا قصيرًا أمام الفصل.

Write an article about my favourite food and give a short presentation in front of the class.

1

كيف تكتب عن طعام مفضل

How to write about a favourite food

A good short article has four parts. Follow the plan and your writing will be clear and easy to read.

PartWhat to writeExample
1. TitleThe name of the foodPancakes with Honey
2. IntroductionWhat it is + when you eat itI eat pancakes every Friday with my family.
3. DetailsIngredients + how you make itWe mix flour, eggs and milk...
4. Your feelingWhy you like itThey are sweet and warm, so I love them.
2

فقرة نموذجية

Model paragraph

📝 نموذج للنسخ

My favourite food is pancakes. I eat them every Friday morning with my family. First, we mix flour, two eggs and some milk. Then my mother fries them in a hot pan. Finally, we put honey and bananas on top. Pancakes are warm and sweet. My brother and I always wait for Friday. They are my favourite food because they are warm, sweet and delicious!

3

كتابة وصفة

Writing a recipe

A recipe has a title, a list of ingredients, and the steps in the correct order.

  1. 1Title: How to Make Fruit Saladالعنوان: كيف نعمل سلطة الفواكه
  2. 2Ingredients: apples, bananas, yogurt, honeyالمكوّنات: تفاح، موز، لبن، عسل
  3. 3First, cut the apples and the bananas.أولًا، قطّع التفاح والموز.
  4. 4Then, put the fruit in a bowl.ثم، ضع الفاكهة في الطبق.
  5. 5Next, add some yogurt and honey.بعد ذلك، أضف بعض اللبن والعسل.
  6. 6Finally, mix everything and serve it cold.أخيرًا، اخلط كل شيء وقدّمه باردًا.
